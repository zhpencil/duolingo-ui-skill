Type

Buttons

The signature control: a 48px face, a 13px radius, and a solid 5px edge in a darker shade of the face. Pressing translates the face down by exactly the edge height and removes the shadow, so the button appears to be pushed into the page. No blur, no gradient, no ripple.

Icons

58 symbols in one sprite. The nav and reward icons keep the flat multi-fill style of the Figma library; the UI glyphs at the end are single-colour and draw with currentColor. Every symbol has its internal ids prefixed, so multiple copies on one page cannot collide.

Overlays

Sheets rise from the bottom edge over 280ms with a 20px top radius and a 44×5 grabber. The scrim is a flat 50% black, never a blur. Dialogs scale from 0.92. Toasts sit above the home indicator and auto-dismiss after 2.4s. Use the controls and watch them stay inside the phone: duolingo.js scopes fixed overlays to the nearest .duo-phone.

Rules that keep it Duolingo
  • Edges are solid offsets. A card gets 2px, a button gets 5px, a node gets 7px. Never a blurred drop shadow, never a gradient on a control.
  • Press means travel. The face moves down by the edge height in 60ms and the edge disappears. Do not scale, fade or ripple.
  • Two pixels everywhere. Borders, dividers, progress gloss and path rings all use 2px steps. The only hairline is the bottom-nav top border.
  • Uppercase means action. Buttons use 16/800 with +0.8px tracking. Body copy is sentence case and never bold unless it is a title.
  • One accent per zone. Green is the next action, blue is information and links, red is danger and hearts, gold is reward, purple is the path and premium, pink is a special event.
  • Illustration is optional. Screens must still read with the mascot box removed; the kit ships a grey placeholder on purpose, because the characters are Duolingo trademarks.
